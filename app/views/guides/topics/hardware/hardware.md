The hardware flow can be a little confusing! **here's how it works:**

## 1. Design hardware projects

The first step to making hardware projects is to make a design for your project! Once you create a new hardware project, it'll show up in your projects list!

To get rewards from Stardance, you'll need to track your time with [Lapse](https://lapse.hackclub.com) - it records a timelapse of you building and files the time against a Hackatime project. We set that Hackatime project up for you as soon as your project becomes a hardware project, so it'll be waiting for you to pick in Lapse.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019ed75a-21f7-7c08-a97c-1c6e553a0b40/paste-1781729468716.png)
_^^ hit this button to record your progress!_

Once your design is done, you'll need to submit it to 1 of 4 tiers to get funding! You can read more about that [here](/guides/tiers)

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019ed761-78c0-7d42-85c2-66ae7660575b/paste-1781729949474.png)
_^^ hit this button to get your funding!_

Different tiers fund different amounts - pick the one that matches what you're making.

## 2. Build your hardware projects, get Stardust!

Once you finish building your projects, record a demo video and ship it! You'll get 5 stardust for every hour you spent building your hardware project!

_we'll have different ways to earn more stardust later, including making a video!_

## 3. Spend your Stardust in the Shop!

Once you get Stardust, you can spend it in the [shop](/shop) to get awesome rewards!

Have any questions? Ask in #stardance-help on Slack!
